# Task 4 – Netflix Release Trend Visualization

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Netflix titles by their **release year** to explore how the platform's content catalogue is distributed over time. The goal is to organize release-year information, visualize the trend, and identify patterns that can support content planning and business decisions.

## Task Description

**Task 4 (Medium) – Netflix Release Trend Visualization**

Analyze and visualize content growth trends using release-year data.

## Task Workflow

1. **Organize release-year information** – Review the release-year field and prepare it for time-based analysis.
2. **Create trend-based PivotTables** – Summarize the number of Netflix titles by release year.
3. **Build line charts** – Use line charts to display how the number of titles changes across release years.
4. **Identify growth patterns** – Examine periods of increase, decline, or slower growth.
5. **Generate trend reports** – Present the findings and explain what they may mean for content planning.

## Dataset

The analysis uses the Netflix titles dataset, which contains **8,790 records**. The `release_year` field is used to group titles by the year they were released.

> **Important distinction:** Release year is not the same as the date a title was added to Netflix. This analysis describes the release-year distribution of titles in the dataset; it does not, by itself, show the exact rate at which Netflix added content to its platform.

## Key Analytical Questions

### 1. What did I analyze, and what question was I trying to answer?

I analyzed the distribution of Netflix titles by release year. The main question was: **How is the catalogue distributed across release years, and what patterns can be observed over time?**

### 2. What specific finding did the analysis reveal?

The release-year PivotTable and line chart show how title counts vary across the years represented in the dataset. The highest and lowest years, as well as periods of increase or decline, should be identified from the final PivotTable and checked against the chart before reporting exact years or figures.

This README intentionally does not state an unverified peak year or year-on-year growth percentage.

### 3. What action could the findings support?

The trend analysis can help decision-makers understand which release periods contribute most to the catalogue and where the distribution is thinner. It can support further investigation into content acquisition, catalogue balance, and programming strategy.

However, release-year counts alone do not establish audience demand, viewing performance, profitability, or the number of titles Netflix added in each year. Those questions require additional data.

## Business Insight

A release-year trend is useful for understanding the age profile and distribution of titles in a content catalogue. It can help guide further analysis of catalogue balance and content strategy. To measure Netflix's actual content additions over time, the `date_added` field should be analyzed separately.

## Conclusion

This task demonstrates how Excel PivotTables and line charts can turn release-year data into a visual trend report. The analysis provides a foundation for understanding the distribution of Netflix titles over time and for asking more focused business questions about catalogue planning.
